Depression: Drugs With FDA Adverse Event Reports

437 medications where depression is among the most-reported events, ranked by number of reports.

These are medications for which depression appears among the most-reported events in FDA adverse event reports. A report does not mean the drug caused the event. Report counts largely reflect how widely a drug is used and how often events are reported; they cannot be used to compare or rank drugs by risk. This list shows drugs where this event is among their top reported reactions, not every drug ever associated with it. This information is not medical advice.
